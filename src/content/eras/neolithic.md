The Boyne Valley monuments are not isolated objects. They are part of a designed landscape where sightlines, mound placement, and river movement all reinforce ritual use.

In Bóinne, this era can connect Newgrange, Knowth, and Dowth notes, so one discovery automatically builds context for another.
